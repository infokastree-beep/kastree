## 2. Description of the Service

Kastree is a **B2B software-as-a-service** product for accounting practices and
similar professional users. In outline, the Service allows you to:

- create client and company records within your organisation;
- upload trial-balance files (Excel or CSV);
- review and confirm account mappings to canonical statement lines;
- generate management-style statements (SOPL, SOFP, SOCIE), variance analysis,
  risk flags, performance overview, and related commentary or Ask features where
  enabled;
- export statement packs (for example Excel, PDF, or CSV).

**The Service is for internal review and analysis only.** It is not a
statutory financial statement, not an audit tool, and not intended for
regulatory filing (see Section 7).

We may change, improve, or discontinue features of the Service with reasonable
notice where practicable. We do not guarantee uninterrupted or error-free
operation.
